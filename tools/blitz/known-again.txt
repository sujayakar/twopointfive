# PROCESS's on purpose, about the Rust module or a pure function, and no game's: nothing here is a rule's to read. What a game leaves behind is put
# back by a start (tools/qa/fresh.ts), not forgiven here.

^g\.m\.character\.at0	a memo: a clip's pose at its first frame, a pure function of the clip, filled as clips are first used (src/game/character.ts AT0)
^g\.m\.netquietfeed\.netfeed\.live$	two switches' AND, written at the top of every tick: before a game's first tick it reads as the process's last tick left it, and the first tick writes it
^g\.m\.scene_collision\.(anytime|both|priced)\.	a tool's switch held with its counts of the questions the module answered (src/scene/collision.ts): a setting, left alone by a start
^g\.m\.kit\.row_cache	a memo: the kit screen's rows, keyed by the switches that shape them (src/game/kit.ts rowCache), filled as a mission's kit is first asked for
^g\.m\.kit\.team$	a setting: the team size the Team & kit screen is set to, as the last mission's loadout left it (a mission that brings no team does not write it)
