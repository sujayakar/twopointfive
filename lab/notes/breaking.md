# Walls that break

What changes (scene/breach.ts, lab.ts `blow`, scenegpu.ts `change`)
- A box that a jagged ring cuts becomes the wedges round it: the box again with three more planes each. Nothing that draws or traces learns a new kind of thing. Marks stay where they were. What was inside falls out as bodies of the same boxes, cut about points, and crumbs.
- Numbers of what stands never change: what is gone is made nothing where it lay (its node stays, empty), what came lies in spare slots with a small tree of its own after the old one (a walk goes on into it). So histories of everything else go on.
- What is left of a wall says `stays`: rays of any width meet it (a wedge is small, but parts rooms).
- Told at once: what is solid, the torch's short rays, the air's faces, smoke's. On the card: what is joined (only the slices a hole is in), where lamps reach (eight frames, a share of the lamps each; what opens only adds), an opening for the sky if one side is out of doors.
- Light that was kept is told to count for less within 9 m for 45 frames, as when a door moves.

Not done: cells beside a wall keep the side they were measured to hold; floors and ceilings do not break; people's way-finding; a wall broken three times over runs out of planes (eight); whole panes go.
