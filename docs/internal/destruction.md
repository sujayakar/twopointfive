# Destruction under sustained fire — stage 3b (lane `feat/destruction`, 2026-08-10)

Branch off `28ca610` (main after the image-quality merge). The maintainer's asks, in order: *"a bit more destruction (like we did with the GPU
particles) when going full auto with the FN2000 … maybe not realistic / maybe won't look good"* → *"stress test how much chaos we could do
(Control!)"* → the **STEALTH-FIRST / LOW-VELOCITY principle** (*"do the slow, methodical stuff great … rather than compromise that for making the
chaos work"*), the **no-unbounded-per-frame-work rule**, Control's bounded model (per-material fragments, ~200-object cap, flying chunks demoted to
sprites under load) and *"IF stealth goals hold, a kinetic mode is welcome"*; and the image-quality program's **measured chaos budget**
(docs/internal/image-quality.md §4: trail is set by shadow hardness × contrast, not speed; nothing trails under the ceiling panels; 64 tumbling
shadow-casting debris boxes cost +2.0 ms at high with no boil → ≤ 64 rigid debris live at ultra / high, ≤ 32 below; prefer particles / dust for volume).

Page: docs/review/destruction.html (self-contained). Probes: `bun run tools/qa/probes.ts destruction`. Rigs: `tools/qa/destr-scenes.js` (`__ds`).

## What to try first

1. **Tab ▸ Sandbox ▸ chaos** is the one knob (0.5 default; the rows under it are the caps). Draw the FN2000 (2), Z for AUTO, stand in a cubicle
   opening and hold the trigger across the desk: the first rounds crack the monitor dead and put the paper stacks in the air, the second half-second
   raises gypsum dust the panel light shows and knocks the binders / phone / mug off, and if you keep it up for a second and a bit a ceiling tile lets
   go overhead and lands with a knock the floor hears. Let go and it all settles: paper on the desk and the floor, chips everywhere, the housing on
   its face, the neck stub standing, the tile where it fell, holes and shreds in the partition. Restart (Shift+Enter) puts every bit of it back.
2. **Do the same into the corridor plaster where a torch is looking** (`__ds.dust()` stages it: `docs/shots/destr_dust_*.jpg`): the beam cuts a shaft
   through the dust the rounds knock out of the wall — that dust is smoke-solver density, so it is lit by the froxels like the canister's plume and
   the guards' sight lines really lose you in it (`__ds.sight()` numbers below).
3. **A stun charge** anywhere under the ceiling (4, click) clears the desk over it, throws the papers within a couple of metres up and drops the tile
   above; the panel button 'bang at cursor' does the destruction without the flash.
4. `chaos 0` and the office is exactly the office it was (probe-held); `chaos 1` and it all comes twice as readily and things leave the desk harder —
   the kinetic-mode seed inside the same caps.

## Design as built

| piece | how |
|---|---|
| **the knob + the model** (`game/destruction.ts`) | `tune.chaos` 0‥1 (default 0.5, `twopointsix.chaos`; `chaosOf(g)` = 0 while `touring`). Rounds — anybody's: `combat.ts fireWeapon` calls `g.destr.round(shooter, hitP, dir, hit, def)` right after the marks + `impactFx` it always made — and stun charges (`g.destr.bang(at)` after `bangScorch`) pour **HEAT** into a 2 m grid decaying with τ = 2 s; a hit's **ESCALATION E = heat × 2 × chaos** gates what happens on top: E ≥ 1 damage decal + paper conversion + a screen's death · E ≥ 1.5 bigger material bursts + a dust puff into the smoke · E ≥ 4 knock-offs + chair spin · E ≥ 9 a ceiling tile. At chaos 0.5 that is: the first round; the second inside two seconds; the fifth of a full-auto string (never at a deliberate 2-round-a-second semi cadence, whose heat saturates at 4); ≈ 1.2 s of AUTO (10 rounds/s saturate at 20) or one stun charge (`bangHeat` 9). At chaos 1 everything comes at half the fire; at 0.25 at double. **chaos 0: both hooks return on their first line** — nothing indexed is touched, no counter moves (`destruction-identity`). |
| **the index** (`Destruction.index`, once at construction, after the targets + devices exist) | Destructible statics found by SHAPE, never by name (the level file carries none for them): **paper** = a NoShadow slab ≤ 3 cm thick, pale (lum > 0.7), 8–20 cm across, on furniture (19 in the office: the `papers()` pairs, magazines, the sign-in book's page); **screen** = an exact `screenOff`-albedo sheet ≤ 12 mm thin (19 desk monitors + the reception's — lit ones are also `LightTarget`s of group `monitors`) with its **housing** paired (the dark slab ≥ as wide, same yaw, within 8 cm behind); **tv** = the big ones (the conference TV — crack + death, never knocked); **clutter / binder** = small (max half ≤ 0.17 m, volume ≤ 0.02 m³) non-emissive non-fixture statics standing 0.7–1.6 m up inside the building — mugs, phones + handsets, the water jug and glasses, the desk fan's parts, plant pots, the photo frame, the three binders, the wall clock … (28 + 8); the monitor's foot and neck (small dark parts right under a housing) are excluded; things standing on / against each other (a pot and its plant, phone + handset) form a **group** knocked together on one cap slot. Bucketed into the 1 m grid for the near queries. |
| **paper → sheets** | A stack within `paperReachM` 0.75 of a hit (× 0.6‥1 with E) and not through the partition the round stopped in, or whose column of air the round's segment crossed (closest approach of the ray to the stack: within its footprint + 6 cm, up to 35 cm over it — the round itself never meets a NoShadow slab), or within 2.6 m of a stun charge in line of sight, is **converted**: its box hidden (`flags |= NoPrimary | NoShadow`, remembered in `hidden`, restored by `reset()`), `sheetsPerStack` 26 × its size × (0.7 + 0.6·chaos) `sheet` particles emitted off its top in a cone away from the hit and up (speed 1.6 + 0.5·E, life 60 s, width = the stack's short side, aspect its long ÷ short, tumble 5 + 4·chaos rad/s, normal jitter 0.55) + a small puff of the dust that lay on it. Cap `papersPerSec` 6. The reverse (a settled sheet turned back into a thin static so some litter persists as geometry) is NOT built: it needs an asynchronous GPU → CPU readback of resting sheets that would break the module's determinism for little — the sheets themselves lie there for their 60 s life (the LONG ring recycles the oldest before that only under load). |
| **the `sheet` particle** (`render/shaders/particles*.wgsl` kind 4, `render/sheetModel.ts` the CPU reference) | State = the 64 B every kind had + one row: octahedral normal (2 × snorm16), angular velocity (3 × f16), aspect (f16). **Air**: v.y −= g·dt; split v into the slip THROUGH the sheet vn = v·n and ALONG it; through-slip decays e^−(KN_LIN 1.5 + KN_Q 9·|v|)·dt (the flat plate's normal force ∝ |v|²·sin α, so a GLIDING leaf gets lift; face-down terminal ≈ √(g / 9) ≈ 1 m/s like an 80 g/m² sheet), along-slip decays e^−(KT 0.5)·dt (nothing); **flutter torque** α = FLUT 25 · (−vn) · (vt × n) — the normal force acting at the centre of pressure a quarter chord AHEAD of the centre pitches the leading edge UP: the glide steepens into a stall, the slide reverses down the new slope, the leaf swoops back (2.4 reversals on average per kicked leaf; a hard kick tumbles it end over end instead); ω damped 1.8/s and kept ⊥ n; n turned by ω; a per-leaf in-plane spin ≤ 3 rad/s × its speed. **Ground**: `traceClosest` from where it was to where it goes with a pad = its leading edge (up to half its length edge-on, paper-thin flat-on); an up face (n.y > 0.6) kills the through-slip, rubs the slide × 0.6, turns it 70 % of the way flat and, under `sleepV` 0.28 m/s, lays it exactly flat 1–6 mm off the surface (a hair of stacking order per slot) and puts it to SLEEP like a chip (support re-tested every 16th frame: shove the desk and they fall); a wall lets it slide down half aligned. **Light**: `sheetLight` — ONE pass over the lights scoring FRONT (toward the eye) and BACK irradiance behind the reach masks, the three strongest shadow-rayed (origin nudged toward the light: either face may be the lit one), the rest at 35 %; shade = albedo/π × (front + TRANS 0.3 × back + the dice's ambient — facing up at rest, the sphere mean in the air). **Draw**: an oriented quad (width × aspect about `sheetFrame(n, spin)`), both faces, its rim antialiased over `fwidth(loc)`, a per-leaf tone (0.9‥1.02) and 12 % rim darkening so a leaf on a leaf keeps an edge, depth-softened against the scene like a chip, reactive weight 1 in flight and 0.15 at rest (litter keeps its TAA history). **No shadow**: a splat, not a box — a leaf in a torch beam is lit but casts nothing on the desk under it (accepted: 40 shadow-casting quads per stack is exactly the fast small occluder the image-quality program said to keep out of hard light). 200 seeded leaves settle in ≤ 1.9 s from desk height, none NaN (`destruction-sheet`). |
| **damage decals** (`render/decals.ts` kind `'damage'` = code 7, the last free 3-bit code; `decals.wgsl damageMark`) | ONE kind, three LOOKS off floor(param), strength = fract: **0 dent** (metal — racks, cabinets, the bin: a dimple tilted into the normal so its far wall catches the light, crazed pale paint round a darkened centre, gloss-keep down); **1 shred** (fabric — the cubicle partitions `partRed/Blue/Tan` and chair backs: a ragged dark tear along a seeded angle with pale wadding down its middle and frayed fibres round it); **2 crack** (a dark screen: black impact blotch, 5–8 wandering hairlines, a faint violet bruise of dead panel, gloss gone along the cracks). Radius pistol / rifle: dent 3.6 / 4.5 cm, shred 6 / 7.5, crack 8.5 / 11. Cap `decalsPerSec` 12 (the bullet hole is still always there); rides a prop / leaf like every mark. Wood / plaster / concrete get no extra decal — the hole and the chip burst are their damage. |
| **screens die** | A round whose static hit is an indexed screen / housing / tv (any shooter — the round is IN it): its `LightTarget` (if lit) `broken = true` + `lightEvent` + `lightBreak` + a blue-white flash — the very rule the OCP / the player's near-miss always used, so `updateTargets` takes its emissive (and its marking's `markGain`) to 0 the same frame and `repairLights` on restart lights it again — plus the crack decal and a fizz of sparks (22 lit / 8 unlit). At E ≥ 4 a direct hit also knocks the housing (+ its screen) off the neck as one rigid chunk; the foot and the neck stub stay. |
| **dust into the smoke solver** (`SmokeEmitter.kind 'puff'`, `Destruction.dustPuff`) | E ≥ 1.5 on plaster / concrete / wood / cardboard / fabric: `g.smoke.emit({ kind: 'puff', lattice: 'coarse', radius 0.2·√scale, density 3.5 × (1 plaster · 0.7 concrete · 0.5 wood · 0.35 fabric) × min(2, 0.7 + 0.2·E), temperature 0.35, ttl 0.35 s, attack 0.03, speed 0.55 along the face normal + up, push 6, expand 8, prio 0, minVoxels 2, anchor 0.6 m off the wall })` — **optically thin on purpose** (density 11 self-shadowed into a black plume in the first captures; 3.5 reads as pale haze the beams light). It IS smoke: the froxels inject it, the volumetric march draws it, and the guards' `probe.querySegment` sight-line march attenuates through it — nothing AI-side was added. Bounded: ≤ `puffsPerFrame` 2, ≤ `puffsPerSec` 8 (exact sliding window), only while `smoke.budget() ≥ 4`; domains are the solver's own pool of 8 (a puff joins the coarse domain it lands in or claims a recycled one; `params.fullRateDomains` throttles the stepping — the solver's cost stayed flat in every take). A tile drop and a stun charge also shake a puff out of the ceiling grid. |
| **material bursts** (`Destruction.burst`, on top of `particleFx.impactFx`'s recipe) | scale s = min(3, 0.5 + 0.5·(E − 1.5)) × 1.4 rifle: wood / cardboard → 7s **splinters** (chips with aspect 3.2, fresh-wood tint) + 3s dust; plaster → 8s pale chips + 6s gypsum puffs; fabric → 9s **fluff** (small slow dust-kind motes of the covering's tint) + 3s threads; metal → 14s more sparks; concrete → 6s grit + 4s dust; glass → 6s dark chips. All GPU particles, all inside the tier's `particleBirths` cap and the queue's carry-over. |
| **rigid debris** (`game/debris.ts Debris`, `Chunk`) | The few things that must stay GEOMETRY: `tile` (a 58 × 58 × 1.5 cm slab spawned 3 cm under the ceiling with a slight rate), `housing`, `clutter`, `binder` (the static's own box taken over: centre, half extents, yaw, albedo), `chunk`. Flight = the throwables' recipe made box-aware: gravity, the centre ray swept through statics + leaves (`col.raycast`) and furniture (`props.raycast`), met SHORT of the surface by the box's own **support distance** along the hit normal in its current attitude (so a tile lands on its face and a binder on its edge), restitution 0.22, tangential keep 0.55 on floors / 0.8 on walls, each floor touch turns it 45 % toward lying on the face it would tip onto (`layFlat`: the axis most aligned with the normal PER UNIT OF EXTENT — a slab ends on its face, a binder on its cover — shortest arc) and trades some of the knock into a wobble about the contact, what it lands on feels it (`props.applyImpulse` × a nominal mass), a world-floor fallback; SLEEP under 0.22 m/s + 1.2 rad/s on an up face (or after 0.25 s nearly still), laid exactly flat and set exactly on the surface under it; a chunk resting on a chair drops again when the chair moves. Drawn as ONE dynamic shadow-casting box each (`MKEY.debris + serial` for the motion vectors). **The pool** = `settings.debrisCap` (quality.ts `Caps.debris`: 64 ultra / high, 32 medium / low / verylow — the image-quality budget verbatim): a spawn at the cap **evicts the oldest chunk already at rest**; with none at rest it **refuses** and the caller demotes the break-off to a 24-chip burst of its colour (the thing still leaves the desk — its static is hidden either way). Ten minutes at rest retires a chunk quietly. |
| **knock-offs** (`Destruction.knockOff / clutterNear`) | E ≥ 4: every clutter / binder within `knockReachM` 0.45 (+ its half size) of the hit, the item itself if hit, a hit screen's housing; a stun charge takes everything within 1.6 m in line of sight. Velocity = along the round (or away from the blast) at `velLow` 1.0 m/s × (0.8‥1.4), a hop of √vK × (0.9‥1.7), a tumble ≤ 5 vK rad/s — where vK = 1 up to chaos 0.5 and rises to `velHighK` 2.5 at chaos 1 (**the velocity policy**: topple at walking pace by default, kinetic only when asked). Cap `knocksPerSec` 4 (a group rides its leader's slot). A chair a round finds is spun (w += ±(4 + 5·chaos)). |
| **ceiling tiles** (`Destruction.tileDrop`) | E ≥ 9 (or a stun charge, forced): the 0.6 m ceiling square over the hit, jittered a square either way; refused outside the building footprint, if that square already dropped this encounter, within `tileGapSecs` 2.2 of the last (rounds), past `tilesPerEncounter` 8, if anything solid stands under the slab there (a wall top, the cable tray, a light panel's box), or if the pool refuses; else a `tile` chunk + 18 crumbs + a 10-puff dust fall + a smoke puff out of the grid. The ceiling slab itself is NoPrimary (the camera looks through it) so no hole is cut — the plenum reads dark from below anyway and a hole would only show in reflections. |

## Impulses — later events kick the litter about again (lane `feat/debris-impulses`, 2026-08-10)

The maintainer, verbatim: *"it'd be cool if subsequent events (stun grenades? nearby bullet impacts?) further kicked paper sheets around"*. Until now
everything a burst put on the floor was painted there: a stun charge going off among two reams of settled leaves moved none of them. Now the
leaves (and the chips, the brass, the dust, the rigid chunks) are pushed again by what happens next.

| piece | how |
|---|---|
| **the record** (`render/particles.ts` `Particles.impulse(pos, radius, strength, { up, bias, kinds, occlude, eye })`, `particles_common.wgsl ImpulseRec`) | 64 B: centre + radius · bias (a direction folded into the push, its length its weight) + strength (= the Δv in m/s a SHEET at the centre gets; the other kinds scale it) · eye + up (the upward share added to the radial push) · kinds mask · seed · flags (1 = occlusion-gated). Queued on the CPU like the emits into a FIXED pool of `MAX_IMPULSES` 16 records (queuing allocates nothing); past sixteen a newcomer within `IMPULSE_MERGE_M` 1 m of a queued one is MERGED into it (strengths summed up to 2 × the stronger, the wider radius, both kind sets, either's occlusion, the stronger one's centre / eye / seed) else the WEAKEST of the seventeen (strength × radius) is dropped — the array never grows. `prepare()` packs them into the TAIL of the same uniform block (`IMP_OFFSET` = 288 + 128 × 112 = 14 624; the block is now header 288 (counts · windows · 10 kind rows · 5 impulse-response rows `ki`) + 128 emit records + 16 impulse records = 15 648 B) and uploads that tail with a second `writeBuffer` ONLY on a frame that carries impulses AND has something live to push; the count rides in `flags` bits 8‥12. Non-finite records are refused (one NaN record would poison every particle in reach). Its own serial seeds it (an impulse never shifts an emit's seed). |
| **the kernel** (`particles.wgsl applyImpulses`, called from `update` before the step, gated `numImpulses() > 0u && !isNew`) | Per live particle of a responding kind: Σ over the frame's records of Δv = strength × gain(kind) × **(1 − d/radius)** × (radial with its downward part dropped + up × upK(kind) × ŷ + bias) × a per-particle raggedness (× 0.7‥1.3 and 0.35 of it sideways along a hashed bearing — a pile does not lift as one clump), skipped under `IMP_MIN_KICK` 0.03 and, for a gated record, when ONE any-hit ray from its eye to a hand short of the particle is blocked (`occluded(…, 0u)`: statics, leaves, props, bodies) or a glass pane stands between (`glassTrace`); then, if the largest single kick clears wake(kind), a RESTING particle is woken (`PB_ASLEEP` cleared — it integrates this very frame), the Δv added, |v| capped at vMax(kind). Per-kind response = the block's `ki` rows (`render/sheetModel.ts IMP_RESPONSE`, live from `Particles.impulseResponse`): **sheet** gain 1 · upK 1 · vMax 6 · wake 0.12 (the reference kind); **debris** chips 0.4 · 0.6 · 4 · 0.25 + another resting angle; **casing** 0.3 · 0.5 · 3.5 · 0.3; **dust** 0.7 · 0.3 · 3 · 0 (shoved along, never sleeps); **spark** 0 (ignored — a pushed streak reads as a second ricochet). ## Residuals, and what a 'kinetic mode' would raise

- Sheets cast no shadows and are not geometry once down (no reverse conversion); a leaf resting over a desk edge lies flat in the air past the edge
  (its centre is supported); overlapping resting leaves at one height merge in tone but for the rim darkening.
- Dust puffs are density only (the smoke field has no per-emitter albedo): gypsum, concrete and wood dust are all the plume's grey-white, told
  apart only by the particle burst's tint in the first half second.
- The ceiling has no tile grid drawn on it (NoPrimary slab), so a dropped tile leaves no visible hole; tiles are generic 60 × 60 slabs whatever is
  overhead (a light panel's square is refused, not shattered).
- Knock-offs are indexed by shape: a few odd statics qualify (the coffee machine's lid, the wall clock, the plant without its shelf) and a few
  plausible ones do not (keyboards are too wide at 40 cm; cartons on shelves too long). Chairs never topple (3-DOF props) — they spin.
- Guards do not react to the *sight* of wreckage (a scattered desk is not evidence) — only to what they hear land and to what the dust hides.
- Cost at chaos 1 in the loud minute is +2‥3.5 ms union at high, most of it the direct pass tracing shadow rays through dust domains: a KINETIC
  mode that wants sustained chaos everywhere would want (a) a per-domain 'thin medium' hint so shadow rays skip puff-only domains under a density
  floor, (b) the debris pool raised toward the measured 64-mover budget with break-offs of authored fragments (Voronoi-cut partitions, drywall
  panels) rather than whole statics, (c) chairs / monitors as full 6-DOF bodies, (d) sheets and chips as occluders in the mesh-shadow BVH at ultra,
  (e) the reverse paper conversion with a deterministic readback schedule, (f) guards reading wreckage as evidence (a 'disturbed' room raises
  suspicion like an open door does).
